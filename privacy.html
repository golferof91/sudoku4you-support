Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Sudoku4you (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a simple Sudoku puzzle game. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

Information We Collect

Sudoku4you does not collect, store, or share any personal information. We do not require an account, and we do not track your activity.

The app may use Game Center (provided by Apple) for leaderboards and achievements. Any data shared with Game Center is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or contact information
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not use analytics or advertising trackers
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties

Children’s Privacy

Sudoku4you is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact
